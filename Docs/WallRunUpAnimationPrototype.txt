UPWARD WALL RUN - ARMS-BACK PROTOTYPE

Asset: /Game/Showcase/AnimationExperiments/WallRun/AA_WallRun_Up_ArmsBack_Prototype
Source: Manny/MM_Run_Fwd (original unchanged).

Uses the supplied running reference's arms-back silhouette, adapted for
upward travel: both arms extend down/back with open hands, the torso leans
away from a front wall, knees alternate upward, and soles face the wall.
Ground foot lift is mapped into recovery away from the vertical surface.

The clip is in place. The movement component must supply upward translation.
The wall is component +Y, corresponding to actor +X with mesh yaw -90.
This is an animation-only experiment, not connected to the gameplay graph.
No plugin source changes, no Prototypes migration, and no Git commits.

Scripts/create_wall_run_up.py authors the clip.
Scripts/preview_wall_run_up.py opens it and checks saved pose data.
Saved/WallRunUpPrototypeReport.json records authoring.
Saved/WallRunUpPrototypeValidation.json records root/loop/knee checks.

Still needs review against a real wall for speed matching, contact distance,
toe penetration, entry/exit blends, and the desired amount of body lean.
